abstract: 'Now-a-days all industries demand lightweight strong material components. Here the composite materials play a crucial role. Since early industrial evolution Cast iron (CI) has used for manufacturing majority of components but due to the Cast iron components weight, many industries particularly automobile industries failed to achieve the expected efficiencies and outputs. Later on, aluminum arose as the better material option. This paper deals with a completely new aluminium alloy. To achieve this, aluminium (AA6061 alloy) was reinforced with rice husk ash and alumina which improve the properties of the composite so formed and the composite was synthesized using stir casting method and different samples with different reinforcement percentage by weight were fabricated and their mechanical properties were tested and compared.'
